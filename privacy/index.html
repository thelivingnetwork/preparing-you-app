Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 June 2026

Preparing You ("we", "us", "the app") is a faith-based companion that helps members walk through a personal journey of preparation, connect with a Personal Contact Minister, take part in gatherings, and message one another privately. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. We collect only what we need to provide the app, and we never sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address — used to create and secure your account and to send you service messages.
  • Password — stored only in securely hashed form by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password in plain text.

Profile information

  • Your name, country, state or region, and time zone.
  • An optional profile photo you choose to upload.

Your journey

  • Your progress through the books, audio and steps within the app, so the app can show you where you are and what comes next.
  • Your relationship with a Personal Contact Minister (the person you choose to walk alongside you), if applicable.

Messages

  • The content of messages you send to your Personal Contact Minister and other members. These are protected with end-to-end encryption — they are encrypted on your device and can only be read by you and the person you are messaging. We store only the encrypted form and cannot read your conversations.
  • Encryption keys — your public key and an encrypted (password-protected) copy of your private key, which make end-to-end encryption possible across your devices.

Notifications

  • If you allow them, a push notification token for your device, so we can let you know about new messages and gatherings. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device or browser settings.

Approximate location (optional)

  • If you use the daily Almanac feature, the app may use your approximate location to show local details such as sunrise, sunset and weather. This is processed through weather and geocoding providers (see below) and is not used to track you.

Technical information

  • Basic information needed to run the app reliably and securely, such as your device/browser type and app version. We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the core features: your journey, your Personal Contact Minister, gatherings, and private messaging.
  • To send you service communications, such as gathering reminders and important notices, in your local time zone.
  • To keep the app safe, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We rely on your consent and on the legitimate need to provide the service you have asked for. We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

End-to-end encrypted messaging

Messages between members are end-to-end encrypted. Your messages are encrypted on your device before they are sent, and are only decrypted on the recipient's device. Because we hold only the encrypted form, we are not able to read the content of your private messages, and neither is anyone who might intercept them in transit.

Service providers we use

We share limited information with trusted providers who help us run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Render — application server hosting.
  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery.
  • EmailJS — sending service emails (such as notifications and reminders).
  • Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud — weather and location lookup for the Almanac feature.
  • jsDelivr — delivery of standard software libraries used by the app.

Data retention and deleting your account

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You are always in control: you can permanently delete your account at any time from your profile within the app (Profile → "Delete my account"). This permanently removes your profile, photo, journey progress, messages, encryption keys and notification settings, and cannot be undone.

If you would like help deleting your account or have questions about your data, contact us using the details below.

How we protect your information

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage of credentials, and end-to-end encryption for private messages. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit access to only what is necessary.

Children's privacy

Preparing You is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

International users

The app and its providers may store and process information in countries other than your own. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information continues to be protected in line with this policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your profile and delete your account directly in the app, or contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
