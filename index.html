Before You Enter

This is a fellowship of charity and free will.
By entering, you walk by these few guidelines.

A house of charity, not commerce

No soliciting, selling, advertising, or fundraising. We gather to serve, not to profit.

Honor one another

Speak with grace. No harassment, slander, or contention — treat every member as family.

Your messages are private

Private messages are end-to-end encrypted — sealed on your device with a key only you hold, unlocked by your password. We cannot read them, and your information is never sold or shared.

Come of your own free will

Participation is voluntary — no coercion, no obligation. You may step away at any time.

Walk in good faith

Be honest, keep confidences, and protect one another.

Tapping Accept & Enter means you agree to these guidelines.

Becoming a Contact Minister

You have walked the preparation and been welcomed by a minister.
Now you may walk it beside others.

The Contact Minister is not a position of power, but of service.

Service, not authority

A Personal Contact Minister holds no office or power over anyone. You do not rule, command, or judge — you simply serve, walking beside those who come as an equal in the family.

Faith, hope, and charity

You give freely and expect nothing in return. The work is unpaid and uncoerced — offered in charity, kept by faith and hope.

Greet and welcome

You greet those who arrive, help them feel at home, and confirm they come of their own free will — never pressing, never gatekeeping out of pride.

Help them choose their own

When someone is ready, you help them choose their own Contact Minister freely. No one is bound to you; every member chooses for themselves.

Connect and protect

You keep members' contact details in confidence — connecting people while protecting what is entrusted to you. Their information is never sold, shared, or used for any purpose but service.

Walk as a team

You serve alongside other ministers, sharing the work and holding one another accountable in charity and good faith.

Tapping Agree & Volunteer means you accept this covenant of service and wish to be listed as a Personal Contact Minister.
