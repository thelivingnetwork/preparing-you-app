PREPARING YOU — Narration script (~5 minutes)

[Slide 01 · 5s] Preparing You — a guided journey that prepares each person, step by step, and never alone, to enter The Living Network.

[Slide 02 · 13s] The Living Network is a community built on trust. Preparing You is its threshold — a gateway, not a gate. It makes sure that everyone who enters has been genuinely prepared, and personally accompanied, before they arrive.

[Slide 03 · 17s] Most onboarding fails in predictable ways. Self-paced courses get skimmed — people click next without absorbing anything. Anonymous sign-ups arrive unprepared, and unknown to anyone. And with no relationship, there is no accountability — no one walking beside them.

[Slide 04 · 17s] Preparing You answers all three. It is guided — one clear next step at all times. It is sequential — each stage is gated, so the foundation is actually laid. And it is accompanied — a real person walks with every applicant. Readiness is earned. And then it multiplies.

[Slide 05 · 8s] At the heart of the app is the journey: a single, evolving priority tile that always shows the one next thing to do — across five stages.

[Slide 06 · 17s] Stage one: watch. Three short films, watched in order. Each one unlocks only when the previous finishes, and seeking past unwatched portions is disabled — so the foundation is truly laid, not clicked through. The orange tile shows exactly where they are.

[Slide 07 · 16s] Stage two: choose a companion. Now they select a Personal Contact Minister — a real person who will walk the rest of the journey with them. Ministers nearby are surfaced first, and this step opens only once the films are complete.

[Slide 08 · 15s] Stage three: the Five Books, in order. This is formation, not information. Each book's audio unlocks the next, completion is tracked for each person, and it is walked alongside their minister — never crammed.

[Slide 09 · 14s] Stage four: join The Living Network. Entry is granted, not grabbed. The minister who walked with them confirms their readiness, and a personal hand-off link appears — so they arrive known, and prepared.

[Slide 10 · 14s] Stage five: become a guide. Those who are granted access can themselves become ministers — walking the next person through the very same journey. The path multiplies.

[Slide 11 · 6s] That is the journey. Now, a quick tour of every function that supports it.

[Slide 12 · 11s] A calm front door — simple, secure sign-in, installable as a phone app, landing every user straight on their next step.

[Slide 13 · 11s] The introduction videos — three films in sequence, with anti-skip playback that records true completion, and clear visual locks.

[Slide 14 · 12s] Choosing a minister — browse those available by region, elect one, and they accept or decline. The result is a committed pairing for the journey.

[Slide 15 · 11s] Messaging and calls — a private, direct line to your minister, with file sharing, one-tap video calls, and push notifications.

[Slide 16 · 10s] The Five Books — audio overviews gated in order, plus full PDFs and audiobooks for deeper study.

[Slide 17 · 9s] The topical library — a searchable A to Z of articles and videos, always available alongside the journey.

[Slide 18 · 11s] Paul, an AI study companion grounded in the source books — ask anything, and the answers draw straight from the text, in your language.

[Slide 19 · 10s] Notifications keep everyone in step — a cross-device bell and web-push for replies, reminders, sign-offs, and announcements.

[Slide 20 · 10s] The weekly townhall — a live, host-moderated gathering, automatically recorded, giving the community a shared rhythm.

[Slide 21 · 10s] And the hand-off — a personalised entry link after sign-off, details pre-filled, so crossing the threshold is seamless.

[Slide 22 · 6s] Behind it all are the tools that keep the gateway trustworthy.

[Slide 23 · 13s] Administrators see everyone's progress at a glance, and can clear the deck — resetting the whole process for a clean start, while admins and seed ministers stay exempt, so the chain survives.

[Slide 24 · 11s] They manage the ministers, and designate seed ministers who persist through resets — the trusted few who bootstrap the chain and cover every region.

[Slide 25 · 10s] And they can speak to everyone at once — a single broadcast reaches every user instantly, by bell and by push, with a full history kept.

[Slide 26 · 14s] This is the flywheel. Each person is prepared, then granted access, then becomes a guide for the next — a self-sustaining cycle where trust compounds with every turn.

[Slide 27 · 11s] Preparing You. A guided, accompanied path — built so no one prepares alone, and everyone who arrives is ready. Walk someone home.
